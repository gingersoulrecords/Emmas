Emma's Country Kitchen logo files

Three versions of the logo, each in three colors.

  emmas-logo-box      The full logo: "Emma's", "Country" and "Kitchen" inside the hand-drawn box.
  emmas-logo-no-box   The same lettering without the box.
  emmas-wordmark      "Emma's" on its own.

Colors

  ink     #2E2620   Dark brown, the ink color on the menu. Use this one first.
  black   #000000   For one-color printing, stamps, engraving and vinyl.
  white   #FFFFFF   For dark backgrounds, shirts and bags.

Which file to use

  svg   Websites, and most design and sign software.
  pdf   Printers. Send the pdf unless they ask for something else.
  eps   Older design and print software, and screen printers who ask for it.
  png   Social media, documents and anywhere that needs a picture file.
        4800 pixels wide with a clear background, about 16 inches wide at 300 dpi.

The svg, pdf and eps files are vector art. They can be printed at any size, from a business card to a sign, without getting blurry.
